WHO ARE YOU ONLINE?

You have an identity in the real world. You also have one online.

YOUR ONLINE IDENTITY:

your online identity is every bit of information of you that can be found on the internet

this may include:

>

Personally Identifiable Information

  • Email address
  • Home address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth

Social media platform

  • Facebook page
  • Instagram account
  • Twitter handle
  • LinkedIn profile

Your digital footprint

  • Websites you visit
  • Photos you post
  • Contents you share
  • Comments you make

WHAT'S A

DIGITAL FOOTPRINT?

Digital footprint is a history of all the activity you do online. Any social media posts you make, any websites you visit, any content you make on the internet and any information you share about you online contributes to your digital footprint.

THINK BEFORE YOU POST!

Once you post something online, it can never be erased. Your online history helps the apps and websites to know more about you and they can use this information to serve you better. However, this convinience also introduces significant privacy risks and lasting impacts on you reputation.


Whatever you share on the internet, it can live forever.
